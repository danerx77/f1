HP indicates a POKéMON's vitality.\nIf a POKéMON's HP drops to zero, it\lfaints and is unable to battle.\lSo, the key in battle is to try to\lreduce the foe's HP to zero.\lIf your POKéMON are hurt, restore their\lHP at a POKéMON CENTER or use items.
EXP. Points stands for “Experience\nPoints.” POKéMON gain EXP. Points by\lparticipating in winning battles.\lWhen a POKéMON accumulates sufficient\lEXP. Points, it levels up. The EXP. Bar\lindicates accumulated EXP. Points.\lIt returns to zero upon leveling up.
POKéMON may learn up to four different\nmoves. There are many kinds of moves\lincluding attacks and those with\lspecial effects. The kinds of moves\lthat can be learned vary for each\lPOKéMON. Some moves can be learned\lfrom items.
ATTACK indicates the power of an\noffensive move. The higher this stat,\lthe more powerful the move.\lIt is applied for the following move\ltypes: NORMAL, POISON, GROUND,\lFLYING, BUG, FIGHTING, ROCK, GHOST,\land STEEL.
DEFENSE indicates the resistance a\nPOKéMON has against physical attacks.\lThe higher this stat, the less damage\lsustained from a foe's physical hit.
SP. ATK (Special Attack) indicates the\npower of a special attack move.\lThe higher this stat, the more powerful\lthe special attack move.\lIt is applied for the following move\ltypes: FIRE, WATER, ELECTRIC, GRASS,\lICE, PSYCHIC, DRAGON, and DARK.
SP. DEF (Special Defense) indicates\nthe resistance a POKéMON has against\lspecial attacks.\lThe higher this stat, the less damage\lsustained from a foe's special attack.
SPEED indicates the agility of a\nPOKéMON. If this stat exceeds the foe's\lin battle, the POKéMON can make a move\lbefore its slower opponent.
Lv. (Level) indicates the growth and\nstrength of a POKéMON.\lIn general, when a POKéMON levels up,\lits stats such as HP, ATTACK, and\lDEFENSE rise, making it stronger.\lPOKéMON may learn a new move upon\lleveling up.
POKéMON and moves are classified by\ntype. Every type has advantageous and\ldisadvantageous matchups against\lother types. For example, like a rock-\lpaper-scissors game, the WATER type is\lstrong against the FIRE type and weak\lagainst the GRASS type.
OT stands for “Original TRAINER,”\nthe first TRAINER to have caught the\lselected POKéMON.\lEven if the POKéMON is traded, the OT\lname will remain unchanged.
All POKéMON can be made to hold one\nitem each. Some items will be used by\lthe POKéMON automatically in certain\lsituations. To make a POKéMON hold an\litem, select the item from the BAG and\l“GIVE” it to the POKéMON.
All POKéMON have a special ABILITY.\nThere are many different kinds of these\labilities, some used in battle, some\lelsewhere. The abilities differ from\lspecies to species. Some species may\lhave more than one kind of ability.
Whenever you win a battle against a\nTRAINER, you are given prize money.\lSave up your prize money so you can\lenjoy shopping at POKéMON MARTS!
Moves are also classified by types.\nIf a POKéMON of a certain type uses\la move of the same type, the move's\lpower is boosted. If the move's type\lhas a matchup advantage against the\ltarget, its effectiveness is also\lboosted.
All POKéMON have a personality,\nor “NATURE.” The POKéMON's\lnature appears to affect how the\lPOKéMON will grow upon leveling up.
The ID No. is a special number assigned\nspecifically to you as a TRAINER.\lTogether with the OT name, the ID No.\lis used to precisely identify the\lOriginal TRAINER.\lThe ID No. does not change even if the\lPOKéMON is traded.
PP stands for “Power Points.”\nIt indicates the number of times a\lcertain move can be used in battle.\lLike HP, PP can be restored by visiting\la POKéMON CENTER or using certain\litems.
POWER indicates the strength of an\nattack. The higher this number, the\lgreater the damage that can be\linflicted on the foe!
ACCURACY indicates the probability of\nthe move hitting the foe.\lThe higher this number, the more likely\lthe move will strike the target.
FNT stands for “fainted.”\nA POKéMON faints if its HP is reduced\lto zero. It becomes incapable of taking\lpart in battle.\lRestore a fainted POKéMON at a POKéMON\lCENTER, or use an item.
Ordinary items bought at any POKéMON\nMART are placed in the ITEMS POCKET.\lItems may be used, given to a POKéMON\lto hold, or tossed out as garbage.
Important items that you find or \nreceive from people are placed in the\lKEY ITEMS POCKET.\lKey items may be used, or registered\lfor use instantly with SELECT. Once\lregistered, the item can be used\lwithout opening the MENU and BAG.
POKé BALLS, which are used for\ncatching wild POKéMON, are placed in\lthe POKé BALLS POCKET.\lPOKé BALLS may be bought at POKéMON\lMARTS. They may be used, given to a\lPOKéMON to hold, or tossed out as\lgarbage.

A TM (Technical Machine) is a device\nused to teach a move to a POKéMON.\lThere are many different kinds.\lMoves can be taught only to those\lPOKéMON capable of using them.\lA TM breaks after a single use.

HIDDEN moves are those that are\nespecially important for your\ladventure. To use such a move outside\lof battle, open the “POKéMON” MENU\land make the POKéMON that knows the\lmove use it. A fainted POKéMON may use\la HIDDEN move outside of battle.
Among POKéMON, there are some that\nundergo major changes (evolution) upon\lgrowing to certain levels. Evolution\lcauses a POKéMON to alter form, change\lits name, and perhaps change the moves\lit may learn. Some POKéMON evolve\ldue to reasons other than leveling up.

POKéMON is a name given to describe\nwondrous creatures that inhabit all\lcorners of this world.\lPeople raise POKéMON to be their pets,\luse them for battling, and so on.
